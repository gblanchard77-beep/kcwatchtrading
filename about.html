Built on Relationships, Not Retail

Kansas City's independent watch dealer.

KC Watch Trading was built through collector and dealer relationships in the Kansas City market. We buy selected luxury watches — with a primary sourcing focus on Rolex sport models — and sell across the major luxury brands: Rolex, Audemars Piguet, Patek Philippe, Omega, Cartier, Tudor, Vacheron Constantin and more.

The model is simple: direct communication, accurate representation, and the ability to source difficult references through a network of private sellers and trusted dealers. You deal with the person doing the deal.

Transactions happen in person in the Kansas City area through our office, or nationwide with fully insured shipping.

KC Watch Trading is an independent secondary-market dealer. We are not an authorized dealer and are not affiliated with any watch manufacturer — which is exactly why we can get you the watch without the waitlist.
