Maison KC

Maison KC Parfums

Small-batch extrait de parfum, made in the United States — nine fragrances named for moments of light and time, from Kansas City's independent house of timeless access.

The House

Maison KC Parfums is the fragrance house of KC Watch Trading — built on the same conviction that luxury is access, not ceremony. Each fragrance is produced in small batches in the United States. The collection is named for moments of light and time — Meridian to Nocturne, Aurora to Selene — a day measured in scent rather than hours. Two pairings anchor the house: Meridian & Lumina by day, Nocturne & Selene after dark.

The Extrait Standard

Every Maison KC fragrance is composed at true extrait de parfum concentration — the richest expression of a fragrance, worn close and lasting long. No dilutions, no seasonal flankers, no compromise between the bottle and the skin.
